## rose-gen — rooted petal geometry

Source: https://github.com/tantaneity/rose-gen
Frozen revision: `09d4a42a99853d7124678436ea08e4921989bf79`

`generator.py` adapts the per-petal root/heading/flank organization, rank-based opening, independent transverse cup, and distal-half roll from `Assets/Scripts/Petals.cs` and `Blade.cs`. The Python continuous Hermite sweep, round shoulder boundary, deterministic parameters, nested bud wrappers, and sampling budgets are project-specific adaptations. The natural-garden entry point and shared study surfaces reuse those acknowledged mathematical helpers. No Unity shaders or author media are included as product assets.
